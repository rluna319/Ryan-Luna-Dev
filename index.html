About

McAllen, TX Software Engineer @ La Joya ISD 5 Research Publications

I'm Ryan Luna, a software engineer at La Joya ISD. I build full-stack applications, APIs, and integrations with C#, .NET, and SQL Server, hosted on Azure. Most of my work centers on database design and backend architecture — keeping systems reliable as they grow.

My background is in embedded systems and robotics. In undergrad, I was co-captain and payload lead for UTRGV's Rocket Launchers, where I worked on avionics and sensor systems for high-powered rockets. That hands-on work is what drew me into engineering.

In grad school, I started in hardware security — working with RTOS and RISC-V — then moved into swarm robotics, where my research focused on swarm security and algorithm design. I published five papers along the way. After finishing my master's, I contributed to Canonical's Multipass project and spent a year teaching an intro to programming course in Python at UTRGV before stepping into my current role.

Take a look at my projects and publications below, and reach out if you'd like to connect.
